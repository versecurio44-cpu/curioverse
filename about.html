About CurioVerse

CurioVerse is a small, independent site for curious people. We start with a simple question and follow it as far as the evidence goes.

How we write

We avoid clickbait and exaggeration. Factual articles cite credible sources such as peer-reviewed papers, space agencies and reference works, so you can check them yourself.

Established knowledge

Well supported by evidence and widely accepted by experts.

Hypothesis

A testable idea that researchers are still evaluating.

Speculation

Reasoning that goes beyond current evidence. Interesting, but not a finding.

Illustrations and AI

Any AI-generated or schematic visual is labelled as an illustration. It is never presented as real footage or a real photograph, and it may be simplified or not to scale.
